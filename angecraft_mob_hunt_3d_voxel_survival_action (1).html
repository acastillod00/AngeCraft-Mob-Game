How to Play & Controls
  • Goal: Eliminate the mob quota to summon the Dread Brute Boss, then defeat it before nightfall! Chain kills within 6s for up to a 5x Score Combo.
  • Move & Dodge: WASD Move • Space Jump (hit while falling for 1.5x Critical Hits!) • Shift Sprint • E Dodge Dash.
  • Weapons & Shield: Left-Click Attack / Shoot / Eat • Hold Right-Click Raise Oak Shield (blocks 80% melee, arrow & Creeper blast damage!).
  • Hotbar Selection: Press 1–5 directly, or press Tab to unlock your cursor and click any hotbar slot directly (scroll-swipe disabled so you never mis-switch!).
